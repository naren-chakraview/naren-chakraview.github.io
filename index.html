The Bottleneck

Why 80% of SMB AI Initiatives Stall Before Production

Jumping straight from raw operational databases to LLM copilots or automated workflows creates expensive hallucinations and security leaks. Before your models can reason, your data layer must be engineered for trust.

Risk 01 // Schema Drift

Dirty & Drifting Schemas

Unannounced upstream application changes silently break downstream executive dashboards and corrupt AI retrieval context windows with malformed records.

Risk 02 // Data Silos

Fragmented SaaS & Operational Traps

Critical business context is trapped across isolated Postgres databases, payment systems, and CRMs with brittle 24-hour batch reporting lags.

Risk 03 // Governance & PII

Ungoverned PII & Compliance Exposure

Feeding raw operational tables into embeddings or LLM prompts without automated Silver/Gold PII masking exposes sensitive customer and financial data.

Risk 04 // Cloud Spend

Brittle, High-Cost ETL Plumbing

Unmonitored spaghetti scripts and oversized proprietary data warehouses drain engineering hours and inflate monthly cloud compute bills.
